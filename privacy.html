Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

MegaShare is an Android app that shares files from your phone with other devices on the same local network, in a browser. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Files you share

While sharing is on, MegaShare runs a small web server on your phone. Devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot that open its address can see only what you chose to share (picked files, one folder, or your whole storage), and can download or upload only if you allowed it. Files travel directly between your phone and that device on your local network. They are never sent to the developer, to a cloud service or over the internet by MegaShare.

Sharing stops when you stop it, or by itself after the idle time you set. You can protect the page with a PIN and block any connected device.

Files you receive

Files that others upload to you, or that you download from another phone running MegaShare, are saved in the folder you choose on your device (Download by default).

Activity and clipboard

The Activity screen lists connected devices (their local network address and browser name), transfers and shared clipboard text for your current and last sharing session. This stays on your phone and you can clear it at any time.

Permissions

  • All files access / storage: to share the folders or storage you choose and to save files you receive.
  • Network, Wi-Fi and nearby Wi-Fi devices (location on Android 12 and older, where Android requires it for Wi-Fi features): to find your local address, start a local hotspot, join another phone's hotspot from its QR code and find nearby phones running MegaShare. MegaShare does not use or record your location.
  • Local network (Android 17+): to connect to other phones on your network.
  • Notifications: to show that sharing is on, transfer progress and alerts you enabled.
  • Install apps: to install MegaShare's own updates.

Scanning another phone's QR code uses Google's code scanner from Google Play services; MegaShare itself gets no camera access.

App updates

Once a day, and when you tap "Check for updates", MegaShare asks GitHub for the latest release of the app and downloads it from GitHub if there is one. No personal data is sent with these requests. This is the only time MegaShare uses the internet.

Children

MegaShare is a general utility app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
