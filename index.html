How it works

Three taps, any device

Your phone serves the files itself, straight over your local network. Nothing goes through the internet.

Pick what to share

Choose a few files, a folder or the whole storage, and whether others can download, send you files, or both. Then tap ▶.

Show the QR code

They scan it with their camera or type the short address in any browser on the same Wi-Fi. No Wi-Fi? Start a hotspot from the app.

They download or upload

A clean file page opens: browse, preview photos and videos, download one file or everything as a zip, or send files and whole folders back.

Everything you need

A file server in your pocket, simple as a QR code

Built for quick everyday sharing, and powerful enough to move a whole phone's worth of files.

Works with every device

Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac or Linux. If it has a browser, it can download and upload. Nobody installs anything.

Send, receive or both

Let people only download, only send you files, or both. Each switch (download, upload, rename, delete, hidden files) can change while you're sharing.

Share exactly what you want

A few picked files, one folder, or your whole storage including the SD card. Add or remove files while sharing, or use “Share” from any app.

A real file page in the browser

List and grid views, thumbnails, search in all subfolders, filters by type, a swipeable photo gallery and videos that play and skip right away.

Big uploads, whole folders

Visitors drag and drop files or entire folders, with live progress and speed. Gigabyte videos are fine. Name clashes get “(2)” instead of overwriting.

Download everything at once

Grab a folder or any selection as one zip, made on the fly. No waiting for the phone to pack it first.

Phone to phone, folders and all

If the other phone has MegaShare too, it pulls everything you share in one go, with subfolders, progress and resume. Files it already has are skipped.

Hand over the app itself

Friend wants MegaShare? They scan the “App” QR and install it straight from your phone, with no internet and no store.

You stay in control

An optional PIN, a live list of connected devices with a Block button, and sharing that stops by itself when nobody has used it for a while.

Shared clipboard

Send a link, a Wi-Fi password or a note between your phone and the PC's browser in both directions. No more emailing yourself.

Start from anywhere

A start/stop button on every tab, a Quick Settings tile, and a notification with the link, live stats and “Add files”.

Stunning themes

Light, dark and AMOLED, 12 accent colors with a matching app icon and loading screen, and 10 animated backgrounds, on phones and tablets.

Questions

Good to know

Is MegaShare really free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Does the other person need to install anything?

No. They open the link or scan the QR code with their camera and use their normal browser, on any phone, tablet or computer. If they'd like the app too, they can install it from your phone through the “App” QR code.

Does it work with iPhone, Windows and Mac?

Yes. Anything with a modern browser works: Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and others. Only the phone that shares needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Do I need internet or mobile data?

No. Both devices just need to be on the same Wi-Fi, or one of them on the other's hotspot. Files never go over the internet, so no data is used.

Is it safe? Can strangers see my files?

Only devices on the same network can open the page, only while sharing is on, and they only see what you picked. You can add a PIN, see every connected device and block it, turn off uploads or downloads at any time, and sharing stops by itself when nobody uses it.

Can I share without a password on the hotspot?

Yes. Android doesn't let apps create an open hotspot by themselves, so the Quick hotspot gets a password, but MegaShare shows a Wi-Fi QR code so nobody types it. For an open network, choose “Phone hotspot” and set Security to “None” in your hotspot settings, or keep a simple 8-digit password.

Is there a file size limit?

No. Large videos and whole folders transfer fine, and downloads between two phones with MegaShare can resume if they're interrupted.

How do I install it, and how do updates work?

Download MegaShare.apk on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. MegaShare checks for new versions once a day and can install them by itself (silently on Android 12+), and never while you're sharing.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

MegaShare is published directly as an APK on this site and its GitHub releases page.
